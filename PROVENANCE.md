# Where the data comes from

| Field | Ordinary Form | 1962 Missal |
|---|---|---|
| Season, celebration, rank, colour, optional memorials | [romcal](https://github.com/romcal/romcal) with each country's calendar rules, as used by the [ambo](https://ambo.today) app | The Roman calendar under the 1960 rubrics, from [Divinum Officium](https://www.divinumofficium.com/) via ambo |
| Titles in Spanish and Portuguese | ambo's translations of the romcal titles | Divinum Officium's Spanish and Portuguese texts, via ambo |
| Commemorations | — | Divinum Officium, via ambo |
| Reading citations | Lectionary citation labels from the bishops' conferences' published daily readings (USCCB for the United States), via ambo. Citations only; no reading texts. | Epistle, gospel and lesson references from the Divinum Officium propers (Vulgate numbering). Citations only. |
| Holy days and penance (status) | Each country's norms: USCCB (United States), CNBB (Brazil), CEM (Mexico), the universal law (general Roman). | The same current norms as the Ordinary Form for that country, as ambo does. Ember Days come from the 1962 calendar. |
| Rose Sundays | Gaudete (Advent III) and Laetare (Lent IV) set to rose | Same |
| Images | Original images made for this project | Same |

Known gaps:
- The 1962 data lists **Pentecost in white**; under the 1960 rubrics it is red. A correction is welcome.
- On **Palm Sunday** the 1962 gospel listed is the one at the blessing of palms, not the Passion.
- The 1962 calendar is the universal one for every country here; only the status (holy days, penance) follows the country.
